## A (recommended)
- Resting (default, every shot): "A pair of chunky round toy goggles rests on the top front of her cream beret, just above the teal band and never over her eyes: two round lenses with thick, soft glossy orange rims, clear glassy lenses with a faint pink-gold sheen, joined by a short orange bridge, held by a teal strap that wraps around the beret. The goggles are the only thing on the beret: no starfish clip, no pin, no other ornament. On the teal strap, at her left temple, sit three tiny five-point star lights in a row."
- Star states: "unlit stars are soft matte grey; a lit star is warm glowing gold." Name the count per shot: "0 of 3 stars lit" / "2 of 3 stars lit (the two nearest the lens gold, the outer one grey)" / "all 3 stars lit gold".
- Charging (ritual): "eyes closed, one paw tapping her chest, the other paw a small fist; the stars on the strap light up gold one after another; the goggles stay on the beret."
- Power on: "the goggles are pulled down over her eyes; both round lenses glow soft pink-gold with a small four-point sparkle in each lens and a few gold sparkles floating around the rims; no beams, no rays leaving the lenses."
- Draining (while she looks): "the strap stars go out one by one, 3, 2, 1"; at 0 the lens glow fades and the goggles pop back up onto the beret with a little bounce.
- Empty / power off: "the goggles over her eyes look plain: lenses clear grey with no glow, all three strap stars grey."
- Level 2 first opening (EP07 G03, after a visible kindness): "the three strap stars light one after another in soft rainbow colours instead of gold, just this once; then the lenses glow soft rainbow (pastel pink, peach, lemon, mint, sky blue bands gently swirling) with a small four-point sparkle in each lens; no beams, no rays leaving the lenses." Later level-2 uses: gold stars, rainbow lens glow.
- Level 3 first opening (EP08 K05, after a visible kindness): "the three strap stars light one after another in soft rainbow colours; the lenses glow soft heart-pink with a small glowing heart shape in the centre of each lens instead of the four-point sparkle; no beams, no rays." - POV (any level): two round lens masks; three small stars along the bottom rim go out one by one as the timer drains.
