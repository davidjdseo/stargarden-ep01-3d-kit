## 1. Wardrobe change
- The orange starfish clip is REMOVED; the cream beret's teal band is plain. Goggles rest on the top front of the beret in every normal shot.
- Goggles (design A): chunky round toy goggles, thick soft glossy orange rims, clear lenses with a faint pink-gold sheen, short orange bridge, teal strap around the beret, three tiny five-point star lights on the strap at her left temple.
- Stars: unlit = soft matte grey; lit = warm glowing gold. Every shot names the count ("0 of 3 lit" … "all 3 lit gold").

## 2. Two modes, one switch
| | NORMAL | HERO MODE |
|---|---|---|
| Goggles | up, on the beret | down, over her eyes |
| Lenses | clear, faint sheen | glow soft pink-gold, a small four-point sparkle in each |
| Neckerchief | tied at the front | turned to her back, flutters like a tiny hero cape |
| Body | plain | a soft pink-gold glow outlines her; a few gold sparkles float around her |
| Pose | everyday | feet apart, one paw a small fist, chin up |
| Stars | whatever she has charged (0–3) | drain while she looks: 3 → 2 → 1 → 0 |

Why these three changes: each reuses something she already wears (goggles, neckerchief) or a light, so the plush identity stays, and a 4-year-old with the sound off reads "she switched on" from the silhouette alone (cape shape plus glowing eyes). No mask, no beams or rays out of the lenses, nothing that hides her face for long.

## 3. The switch (transform beat, about 1 s)
1. CHARGE (only when all 3 stars are gold): eyes closed, one paw taps her chest, the other makes a small fist. The stars flare brighter in turn, 1-2-3.
2. "반짝 고글, 켜져라! / Sparkle Goggles, on!" Both paws pull the goggles down. One ring of gold sparkles pops around her, and the neckerchief swings round to her back.
3. HERO MODE holds as long as the stars last. Inside the goggles we see the POV: a two-circle mask, zoomed, soft then sharp.
4. When the last star goes grey, the glow fades, the neckerchief drops back to the front and the goggles pop up onto the beret with a little "뿅".

EMPTY attempt (0 stars): she pulls the goggles down and nothing happens: grey lenses, no glow, no cape swing. She taps the lens like knocking on a door ("똑똑"). That is EP01's fair surprise (H12).

## 4. Rules for keys and takes
- The star count carries over from the previous cut's last state (EP01-SCENARIO.md star column).
- HERO MODE frames always show: lenses glowing, all lit stars gold, neckerchief cape behind her, outline glow. NORMAL frames never show the cape or the glow.
- Sound (no voice line): a soft rising chime on the switch, a tiny "뿅" when the goggles pop back up.
- Never: beams or rays from the lenses, a mask over the face, a new cape object (the cape IS the neckerchief), the starfish clip.
